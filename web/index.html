On the FM-1: the white keys show pictures 1–16, OCT− / OCT+ and the knobs step through them, PLAY runs a slideshow (KNOB 4 sets the pace), SAVE keeps a picture sent without “Keep”, HOME tells you which one it is.

Needs the Mocky firmware on the FM-1 (install it), and Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI with SysEx). Plug the FM-1 straight into the computer. About Mocky · source
