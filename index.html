Mocky

Pictures on the FM-1's screen, sent from your browser. Drop a screen mockup on the page and it's on the FM-1 a moment later, exactly as the screen will show it: check the colours, the sizes, and how the text reads at arm's length, on the real thing.

Version 0.9.1. It installs and uninstalls the way Felucca and X0X do, and the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. Installing is at your own risk.
Send picturesChrome or Edge, with the FM-1 on USB running Mocky. Drop, choose or paste a picture. InstallFrom Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB. Nothing to install on the computer. Download mocky-0.9.1.fwscFor the command-line installer: python3 tools/fm1_install.py mocky-0.9.1.fwsc SourceGitHub, GPL-3.0. Built on Felucca by Hügelton Instruments.

Sending

Any sizePictures are scaled to the screen's 240 × 240: Fit (with a colour behind), Fill (cropped), Stretch, or 1:1. A picture made at 240 × 240 arrives pixel for pixel.
True coloursThe page shows what the screen will show (the screen's 16-bit colour). Dither for photos and gradients, if you like.
KeepKept pictures stay on the FM-1 when it's switched off: 276 KB of room, up to 64 pictures. A flat mockup takes a few KB, a photo about 116 KB.
SeveralDrop several pictures at once and they're sent in order: a whole flow, ready to step through.
Watch a filePick a file and the page sends it again each time it changes. Export from your design tool over the same file and the FM-1 follows.

On the FM-1

White keysPictures 1–16. Their lights show which are kept; the one on the screen blinks.
OCT− · OCT+Previous, next. SELECT, ALGORITHM, PRESETS and KNOB 1–3 step too.
PLAYSlideshow on and off. KNOB 4 sets the pace, 1 to 30 seconds a picture.
SAVEKeeps a picture sent without "Keep" (SAVE blinks while there is one).
HOMEWhich picture this is, and how much room is left.

Mocky starts on the picture you looked at last.

Screen reader

Mocky works with the FM-1 Screen Reader. A picture can't be put into words, but Mocky tells the reader everything else it shows: which picture is on the screen ("picture 3 of 7"), a new picture arriving and whether it was kept, the slideshow, the room left, and what each button did. Open the reader in Chrome with the FM-1 plugged in; it speaks with its own voice, or hands the text to your computer's screen reader.
