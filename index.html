Mocky

Mocky puts your pictures on the FM-1's screen, sent from your browser. Drop a screen mockup on the page, and a moment later it's on the FM-1, exactly as the screen will show it. Use it to check the colors, the sizes and how the text reads at arm's length, on the real thing.

Version 0.9.2. You install and uninstall it the same way as Felucca and X0X, and the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. Installing is at your own risk.
Send picturesOpen it in Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 running Mocky and connected by USB. Then drop, choose or paste a picture. InstallConnect your FM-1 by USB and install from Chrome or Edge. There's nothing to install on your computer. Download mocky-0.9.2.fwscFor the command-line installer: python3 tools/fm1_install.py mocky-0.9.2.fwsc SourceOn GitHub under GPL-3.0. Sending

Any sizeSend a picture of any size, and the page scales it to the screen's 240 × 240. Choose Fit (with a color behind it), Fill (cropped), Stretch or 1:1. A picture made at 240 × 240 arrives pixel for pixel.
True colorsThe page shows you what the screen will show, in the screen's 16-bit color. Turn on Dither for photos and gradients, if you like.
KeepPictures you keep stay on the FM-1 when you switch it off. There's 276 KB of room, for up to 64 pictures. A flat mockup takes a few KB, and a photo about 116 KB.
SeveralDrop several pictures at once, and the page sends them in order. You get a whole flow, ready to step through.
Watch a filePick a file, and the page sends it again each time it changes. Export from your design tool over the same file, and the FM-1 follows along.

On the FM-1

White keysShow pictures 1–16. A key lights when its picture is kept, and the one on the screen blinks.
OCT− · OCT+Step to the previous or next picture. SELECT, ALGORITHM, PRESETS and KNOB 1–3 step through them too.
PLAYStarts and stops the slideshow. Turn KNOB 4 to set the pace, from 1 to 30 seconds a picture.
SAVEKeeps a picture you sent without "Keep". SAVE blinks while there's one to keep.
HOMETells you which picture this is, and how much room is left.

When you switch it on, Mocky starts on the picture you looked at last.

Screen reader

Mocky works with the FM-1 Screen Reader. A picture can't be put into words, but Mocky tells the reader everything else it shows. You'll hear which picture is on the screen ("picture 3 of 7"), when a new picture arrives and whether it was kept, the slideshow, the room left, and what each button did. Plug in the FM-1 and open the reader in Chrome. It speaks with its own voice, or hands the text to your computer's screen reader.

To go back to the official firmware, use "Back to the stock firmware" in the installer, or M-VAVE's own updater, M-UPGRADE, from m-vave.com/download.
